WORKSPACE MANAGEMENT

Email & Communication Setup

Configure how Demo Workspace sends, receives and routes customer communication. The day-to-day Email Inbox stays under My CRM. Individual employees connect their own workspace-specific Gmail / Microsoft sender under My Account.

Separation rule: Workspace routing and company communication settings live here. A user’s personal sending mailbox remains tied to that user + this workspace only, so switching companies never reuses the wrong sender.

Outbound CRM email

The CRM can send customer email through the website mail transport without each rep connecting Gmail. Workspace Owners/Admins control whether employees may use personal connected mailboxes.

No CRM workspace sender configured. Customer-facing email will not fall back to the My Forever CRM platform support address. Employees may use an allowed connected mailbox, or an Owner/Admin can configure a workspace sender here.

This is a CRM-managed sender identity, not a Gmail connection. Actual delivery still uses the site/server mail transport. If you enter a different domain, that address must be authorized by the mail provider for reliable delivery.

Inbound customer email

Current production path: CRM Reply Gateway. Direct Gmail/Microsoft inbox sync stays disabled until the required provider permissions are approved and connected. Nothing here grants inbox-reading access by itself.

SMS / phone provider readiness

Choosing a provider here does not create an account or charge anything. It keeps the communication layer provider-neutral until you choose a carrier.

Shared inbox response SLA

Example: 30 = 30 minutes, 120 = 2 hours, 480 = 8 hours, 1440 = 1 day.
