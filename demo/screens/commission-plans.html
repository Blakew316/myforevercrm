WORKSPACE COMMISSION METHOD

Manual, Automatic or Hybrid

This setting exists in every workspace and every industry template. The template may choose the starting method, but the workspace owner controls the final choice.

Manual: enter the exact commission amount for each person; no bracket or automatic formula is required. Automatic: use commission plans/brackets to calculate payouts. Hybrid: use automatic plans where desired and add exact manual entries for custom deals or overrides.

Changing this setting never deletes existing commission entries or plans. Manual mode simply prevents automatic plans from being applied to new projects until the workspace is switched back to Automatic or Hybrid.

Appointment attendance commissions

Optional, separate from commissions paid when a deal closes. An appointment must take place and be confirmed by an authorized workspace manager or accounting employee. Booking alone never generates a payout. New payouts start as pending for accounting review.

Commission brackets · this workspace

Create reusable commission rules for groups of products. Assign products individually or during price-list import. Unassigned products never inherit a default commission. Nothing here creates or pays commissions.

Optional: use one commission bracket for the whole workspace. Explicit product assignments override the default; an individual product policy takes precedence over both. Applies only to future estimates.

Use a product-specific policy if individual costs differ; the bracket cost reference is shared by all assigned products. Bracket changes affect future estimates, not historical posted commissions. Payouts remain subject to Accounting approval.

Product-specific commission policies

Each workspace keeps its own rules. Choose a catalog item and configure its pool, sales rep share and manager share. A discount reduces the actual selling price used in a margin-based estimate. Estimates are not commission entries, payroll or automatic payouts. Accounting may still create manual commission entries for custom deals.

Create a product in this workspace’s proposal catalog first.

Sales & billing commission review

Review a saved proposal, or an invoice linked to one, using this workspace’s product policies. Prices, quantities, invoice discounts and posted payments inform estimates. Nothing here posts commissions or pays anyone. Unmatched items must be reviewed by Accounting.
