MARKETING LEAD CONNECTIONS

Lead Capture Center

Bring website, Facebook / Meta Lead Ads and Google Ads lead-form submissions directly into this workspace. Marketing leads keep their source/campaign attribution and pass through duplicate protection. When the Lead Routing Engine is enabled, new unassigned leads are routed automatically by your priority rules; otherwise they remain in the protected Unassigned Marketing Leads pool.

MARKETING CENTER

Marketing lead connections & routing

Connect website forms, Meta/Facebook lead ads and Google Ads lead forms to one inbound pool. The Lead Routing Engine can automatically assign them by territory, team, source, service, performance, workload or manager rule.

Lead-source connections

Website endpoints are workspace-specific. Meta Lead Ads use the platform webhook automatically after a Meta Page is selected. Google Ads lead forms use Google's native webhook delivery plus an API recovery sync when a Google Ads account is connected.

In each Google Ads lead form, choose webhook delivery and enter this CRM webhook URL and key. Google sends each submission directly into this workspace. GOOGLE ADS LEAD DELIVERY

Lead Form Sync & Recovery

The webhook is the real-time path. When a Google Ads account is connected, My Forever CRM can also read lead-form submissions from the Google Ads API to recover submissions that were not delivered by webhook.

Google Ads account not selected

Connect Google and select a Google Ads account under SEO & Website Analytics first. Webhook delivery can still be configured independently when you have a Google lead form.

Open Google Ads Leads

The CRM test button creates a clearly marked test lead using the same parser, deduplication, attribution and routing path as a Google webhook. It does not pretend to be a live Google Ads submission.

Marketing lead pool & attribution

Inbound marketing leads use the Lead Routing Engine when it is enabled. Any lead that does not match an eligible route remains unassigned. Source and campaign attribution remain attached to the record.
