For exactly seven-day review periods, the hourly estimate includes an illustrative 40-hour / 1.5× weekly overtime calculation only when all recorded shifts in the period are reviewed. This is NOT jurisdiction-specific overtime compliance; alternative thresholds, daily overtime, workweek alignment, mixed rates, bonuses affecting regular rate, unpaid breaks and shifts crossing period boundaries require independent payroll review. Worker pay categories
